Playing Kutná Hora Solo

Choose Undo immediately after starting a new game to restore your previous game.

Choose one, two or three rivals in New game. With three rivals the game lasts five rounds. With one or two it lasts six. Two-player games use event cards. Events are optional on the larger boards.

Take your turn. Play two cards on each of your first two turns and one on your last. Pick the card half and target, then confirm. With multiple rivals, Play rivals continues until your next card, tax decision or final scoring.

Set up your guilds. Choose your set from the printed setup card and arrange your house rows. The seed assigns the remaining sets and the rivals’ row order.

Build your income. Take Rights to a building, claim a Plot, then use Building. A Mine can also start ore production. The Income action collects the current value of your production. The market prices affect all players.

Follow the grid. Town and mine coordinates are read left to right, top to bottom. Colored dots are printed scoring matches. A striped guild offer can sell Rights while new construction of that guild is unavailable.

Win on points. Patricians score in the last three rounds. The final count adds town matches, mine row awards, final income, reputation and unused items to earned points. Coins break a points tie. Any players still tied for first share the win.

Check public information. Open a tile to inspect its costs and effects. Use Details when the tile is an action target. Each player board shows the full house rewards, production and supplies. Each rival's remaining cards are below the turn controls.

Resume or share a position. This browser keeps your game and up to five recent Undo steps when local storage is available. If another tab advances the save, choose Load saved game to continue from that position. Export a JSON save to move between browsers or share a position. Version 0.1.0 saves keep their existing deck order.

Rival settings. Standard and Quick consider the next player's likely card. Planner tries the replies on a copy of the board, explores several possible draws when choices are close, and looks further ahead for builds and final scoring.

Mincmistr. Choose Play one Plan to see the moves, resources and board spaces changed. The review also shows any automatic round-end effects. Expand Why this move? for the checks and tie-breaks, or tap a rule number to read it offline.

Report a playtest. The journal saves your Playtest note with the game. Use Export playtest report to share the game and note. If autosave is paused, Export game and note in the save warning makes the same kind of copy. Open a report with Import save, then use Load position before this step in the journal if an earlier position is included. Undo returns to the imported position.

Playing on the table. Open Draw only for a shared Plan deck. Draw one Plan for each Mincmistr action card. Rules and reference has the full rules and two printable aids. Use Export deck and Import deck to move the current deck and five recent Undo steps between devices.

Mincmistr levels. Journeyman follows the printed priorities. Apprentice simplifies plot, build and mine choices. Master plays both cards on its last turn in the final three rounds, and Grandmaster does so each round. In three-player games, Master can also double its last turn in rounds 3–6.
