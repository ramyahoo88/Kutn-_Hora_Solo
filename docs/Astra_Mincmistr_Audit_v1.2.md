## Confirmed findings

### 1. The Goals card uses the two-player council comparison

**Medium · Changes a decision and can spend 10 g.** Rules 6.6 and 10 tell a Mincmistr to compare its points per patrician with the best of all opponents for that color. The printed Goals card on p. 7 compares with the human only. The simulator uses the best opponent.

Reproduce with `positions/reference_edge_3p.json`, seed `minc-ref-edge`, or set three players' per-silver-patrician scores to 0/3/3. Seat 0 is the human, seat 1 is acting, and seat 2 is another Mincmistr. Seat 1 has 15 g, a usable Income card, an available silver patrician, and no positive edge in another color. The reference card sees a +3 edge against seat 0 and seats silver. Rules 6.6 and 10 give an edge of zero against seat 2 and call for plain Income. The assertion finds both legal actions and confirms the 10 g difference.

Smallest fix. Replace the Goals card's edge sentence with “Edge = its points per patrician − the best opponent's.” Regenerate the PDF and inspect p. 7. [Reference source](../mincmistr/print/build.py#L79) · [Rulebook](../mincmistr/print/rules.md#L126) · [Policy](../mincmistr/mincmistr.mjs#L62)

### 2. The bonus section sends tile 9's free Mines to the paid Mine rule

**Medium · Conflicting printed instructions for cash and miner supply.** Mincmistr 5.3 tells you to resolve St. Barbara normally. Section 6.5 refers the two bonus Mines to 5.2, which uses a pool miner or buys a permit to recruit. CGE p. 20 instead grants two free Mines, using reserve miners before pool miners. The simulator follows the CGE text.

Reproduce from `positions/free_mine_pool_0.json` and `positions/free_mine_pool_1.json`. The Mincmistr has 20 g and one pelican at tile 9. Resolve the first free Mine. With zero in the pool, applying 5.2 would buy a 4 g permit. With one in the pool, 5.2 would use that miner. The code pays zero and uses a reserve miner in both fixtures. After the first placement there are still 20 g, the original pool count and seven reserve miners. `positions/free_mine_reserve_empty.json` checks the other boundary. The first free Mine uses the last pool miner and the second skips.

Smallest fix. Give extra Mines their own sentence in 6.5. “For each free Mine on St. Barbara 9, use a reserve miner before a pool miner. Pay nothing and use 6.4 to choose and place the tile.” Keep 5.2 for ordinary Mine actions. Apply the change to both copies of the rules and rebuild. [Rulebook](../mincmistr/print/rules.md#L120) · [Bonus policy](../mincmistr/mincmistr.mjs#L219) · [Engine supply choice](../src/engine.mjs#L397)

### 3. The benchmark can label Standard results as Planner

**Medium · Can invalidate a comparison.** On the unmodified audited branch, run `node mincmistr/multisim.mjs solo 3 master planner 1 audit-planner-label /tmp/as-planner.jsonl`, then repeat with `standard` and a separate output file. The three scores, winners and focus seats are identical. The JSON labels them `planner` and `standard` respectively. The branch has the v0.3.0 bot implementation, whose unknown-policy fallback is Standard.

Smallest fix. Validate requested policy names against the imported bot implementation before the run. Add a command using an explicit v0.3.1 checkout for Planner. The audit harness uses that exact implementation and refuses a Planner comparison without a policy checkout. [Benchmark call](../mincmistr/multisim.mjs#L20) · [Fallback](../src/bot.mjs#L386)

### 4. The workload script skips Master and Grandmaster's extra plays

**Low · Understates how many decisions a person handles.** Run `node mincmistr/overhead.mjs master 2`. It reports 30.0 card plays per two-player game. Rule 9 gives Master 33 and Grandmaster 36. `overhead.mjs` creates the requested level but never extends the last turn. The main simulation runner applies the extension and counted the expected plays.

Smallest fix. Call `mincmistrCardsThisTurn` and apply the returned card count before calling `mincmistrAction`, as `multisim.mjs` does. [Workload loop](../mincmistr/overhead.mjs#L10) · [Expected extension](../mincmistr/multisim.mjs#L18)

## Clarifications and optional improvements

- **Ambiguous wording.** Yellow's “weakest mine row” can sound like lowest stars or lowest mine VP. CGE p. 16 and the code count mines. For example, with count 1/2/2/2 and two zero-star mines in the second row, the answer is 2 points per yellow patrician. Say “2 per mine in the row where it has the fewest mines, maximum 4 points; an empty row counts as zero.” [Rulebook](../mincmistr/print/rules.md#L132) · [Scoring code](../src/engine.mjs#L593)
- **Optional print usability.** Keep the full free-right bullet on one page and the multiplayer setup bullets together. Move “skip impossible checks” onto the Card Play reference and use “2+ pelicans” on all 15 Plan cards. Give the multiplayer reference the large-board 2/1/0, 3/2/1, 5/3/2 awards and the maximum 4-point yellow check. A four-player Master table makes 84 Plan flips and five reshuffles with a shared deck, so reducing lookups on the cards matters.
- **Optional teaching clarification.** Say that Grandmaster doubles its last turn in all five or six rounds. Label the `N=0–6` fine-tuning table as two-player data. Explain Journeyman as the baseline with the normal number of plays and Master as the harder starting recommendation for players who match the app's Standard.
- **Balance scope.** The tests covered a human stand-in against all Mincmistrs and compared published summaries for a single Mincmistr among app rivals. Human opponents, physical print alignment, and longer-range attempts to manipulate the bots' commodity income remain open. Fix the two print rules first, then the benchmark validation and workload helper.

## Reproduction

Extract the evidence archive and use Node 24, Python 3 and an exact checkout of the audited branch. The `positions` folder includes the rule fixtures and the complete sampled four-player trace. The JSONL results include seeds, setup, all rotated scores, cash tiebreaks and outcome data. The benchmark writes a replacement output file. Use a scratch path for a new run. [Evidence archive](../audit/mincmistr-v1.2/Mincmistr_v1.2_Audit_Evidence.zip)

```sh
export MINCMISTR_ROOT=/path/to/audited-checkout
export MINCMISTR_PLANNER_ROOT=/path/to/v0.3.1-playtest-checkout
cd /path/to/extracted-evidence
node rules-check.mjs /tmp/mincmistr-positions
node bench.mjs fresh.json /tmp/mincmistr-fresh.jsonl
node bench.mjs confirm.json /tmp/mincmistr-confirm.jsonl
node bench.mjs tactics.json /tmp/mincmistr-tactics.jsonl
node bench.mjs replay.json /tmp/mincmistr-replay.jsonl
python summarize.py /tmp/mincmistr-fresh.jsonl /tmp/mincmistr-confirm.jsonl /tmp/mincmistr-tactics.jsonl
```

The evidence behind the verdict is the verified base build, matching published sample and Master replays, legal completed games across every level and table size, and the two focused printed-rule discrepancies above. The downloadable PDF was inspected directly. Physical play remains the next check after the corrections.
